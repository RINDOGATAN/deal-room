## What it is and when it is used

A California residential lease agreement is a **standard rental agreement** for a home in California. It is the contract used when a landlord rents a home to a tenant, and it sets out the basic terms both sides need to know from the start.

The Dealroom skill covers these subjects:

- **how long you rent**, the length of the tenancy;
- **how much you pay**, the rent;
- **your deposit**;
- **pets**; and
- **what happens if you need to leave early**.

The skill negotiates 7 clauses.

## Who signs it

The lease is signed by the **landlord**, who rents out the home, and by the **tenant**, who will live in it.

## Jurisdictions and languages

Dealroom offers this lease for **California** only, and drafts it in **English** only. It is meant for homes located in California; Dealroom does not offer it for homes in other US states.

For a home elsewhere, Dealroom offers separate residential tenancy skills built on local law:

- an [assured periodic tenancy](/contracts/assured-periodic-tenancy) for England and Wales; and
- a [Spanish residential lease](/contracts/residential-lease-spain), drafted in Spanish.

The landlord and the tenant can use Dealroom together, each stating its preferences, or one side can prepare the lease alone.
