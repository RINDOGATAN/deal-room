## What it is and when it is used

An assured periodic tenancy agreement is the contract for letting a home to a tenant in **England and Wales**. The Dealroom skill prepares it under the **Housing Act 1988**, as amended by the **Renters' Rights Act 2025**, which came into force on **1 May 2026**.

That change matters for anyone used to the older model:

- **fixed-term assured shorthold tenancies are abolished**; and
- **all new tenancies are periodic from the start**, meaning they run from one rent period to the next rather than ending on a fixed date.

A landlord letting a home after 1 May 2026 should therefore expect to use a periodic assured tenancy, and this skill is built for that purpose.

The skill negotiates 6 clauses.

## Who signs it

The agreement is signed by the **landlord**, who lets the home, and by the **tenant**, who will live in it.

## Jurisdictions and languages

Dealroom offers this tenancy agreement for **England and Wales** only, and drafts it in **English** only.

For a home in another place, Dealroom offers separate residential tenancy skills built on local law:

- a [California residential lease](/contracts/california-residential-lease); and
- a [Spanish residential lease](/contracts/residential-lease-spain), drafted in Spanish.

The landlord and the tenant can use Dealroom together, each stating its preferences, or one side can prepare the agreement alone.
