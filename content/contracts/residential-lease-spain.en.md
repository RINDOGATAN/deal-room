## What it is and when it is used

A Spanish residential lease (in Spanish, *contrato de arrendamiento de vivienda*) is the contract under which a landlord lets a dwelling to a tenant who will live in it as their **main home** (*vivienda habitual*). It is the contract used for the ordinary rental of a home in Spain.

The Dealroom skill prepares the lease under the **Ley de Arrendamientos Urbanos (LAU)**, the Spanish law on urban leases. The LAU distinguishes leases of a main home from other kinds of lease, and this skill is built for the main-home case.

The skill negotiates 7 clauses.

## Who signs it

The lease is signed by the **landlord** (*arrendador* or *arrendadora*), who lets the dwelling, and by the **tenant** (*arrendatario* or *arrendataria*), who will live in it.

## Jurisdictions and languages

Dealroom offers this lease for **Spain** only, and drafts it in **Spanish** only. The page you are reading explains the document in English, but the lease itself is produced in Spanish.

For a home in another country, Dealroom offers separate residential tenancy skills built on that country's own law:

- an [assured periodic tenancy](/contracts/assured-periodic-tenancy) for England and Wales; and
- a [California residential lease](/contracts/california-residential-lease).

The landlord and the tenant can use Dealroom together, each stating its preferences, or one side can prepare the lease alone.
