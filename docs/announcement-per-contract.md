## English

**Subject:** Dealroom: pay only for the contracts you use

Hello,

From [start date], Dealroom charges per contract.

What is new:

- Drafting and negotiating remain free, for every contract type.
- You pay once for each contract, when you download it or start the
  signature: $29 or €29, whatever the template. Every template is included.
- If you use Dealroom through an AI agent, the agent pays with prepaid
  credits, sold in packs of ten at 25 percent off. The credits belong to
  your account, so every API key you hold draws on the same balance, and
  changing or revoking a key does not lose them.

What it costs:

- One contract: $29 or €29.
- Agents: a pack of ten credits, $217.50 or €217.50 (25 percent off).

Contracts you started before [start date] stay free, and so do contracts
that were already in signature or signed.

The Dealroom team

## Español

**Asunto:** Dealroom: paga solo por los contratos que uses

Hola:

gratis, igual que los que ya estaban en firma o firmados.
