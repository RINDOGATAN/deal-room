## English

**Subject:** Dealroom: pay only for the contracts you use

Hello,

From today, Dealroom charges per contract.

What is new:

- Drafting and negotiating remain free, for every contract type.
- You pay once for each contract, when you download it or start the
  signature: $29 or €29, whatever the template. Every template is included.
- If you prepare contracts often, the monthly plan gives you unlimited
  contracts for $9 or €9 a month.
- If you use Dealroom through an AI agent, the agent pays the same price per
  contract with prepaid credits, sold in packs of ten.

What it costs:

- One contract: $29 or €29.
- Monthly plan: $9 or €9 a month, unlimited contracts.
- Agents: credits in packs of ten.

Contracts that were already in signature or signed are not affected.
